Ashley's Story

Born from years
of sleep struggle.

This podcast was born from years of sleep struggle. Ashley tried everything — and nothing worked consistently. What finally did was podcasts. Specifically history and science, delivered slowly and without urgency, something interesting enough to quiet a restless mind but calm enough to let it drift. She had two favourites. They came out once a week. The nights in between were long.

So in a frustrated, sleep-deprived act of necessity, she created The Restful Record.

Millions of people struggle with sleep. Like Ashley, they might find that slow, interesting stories about history and science are exactly what they need to finally close their eyes and rest. If you feel burnt out, overstimulated, or just need something to carry your mind somewhere quieter — this podcast was made for you.
